Decision guide · practice sentences

Part 1 · The Tense Decision Guide

STEP 1 · WHEN? Find the time clue.
NOW / ALWAYS (present)
STEP 2 · Is it a habit / fact / state — or in progress now / temporary?
present simple
he climbs · they climb
habit · fact · state verb
every day · usually · always · often · never · on Sundays
know · believe · like · belong · seem
present progressive
he is climbing
happening now · temporary
now · right now · at the moment · Look! · Listen! · this week · these days
BEFORE NOW (past)
STEP 3 · Is there an earlier past — something that happened before another past event?
past simple
he climbed
NO · finished past · story events in order
yesterday · last June · in 2017 · two days ago · then · next · when he arrived
past perfect
he had climbed
YES · the EARLIER of two past events
before · after · by the time · already · because (earlier cause) · when … had already

One climber, four tenses

Every day Alex trains (habit). Right now he is resting (in progress). In June 2017 he climbed El Capitan (finished past). Before that day, he had practised the hardest moves (earlier past).

Before I choose a tense I ask:

  1. When? (find the time clue)
  2. Finished or in progress?
  3. Is there an earlier past?

Grammar Checklist #1

  • Fact, habit, or state verb → present simple
  • Happening now / in progress → am / is / are + -ing
  • One tense marker per verb group
  • No state verbs in -ing (know, seem, rely, depend, belong, believe)

Trap! A time word helps, but MEANING decides. “Today he knows the route” — know is a state verb, so no -ing.
